# Paper Revision Notes

## Story

1. Land-use change can alter local temperature, clouds, and rainfall, but the sign and location of the response depend on the complete environmental and experimental setting.
2. Exhaustively testing many spatial land-use layouts with high-resolution weather models is computationally expensive.
3. ClimateKG turns land-atmosphere papers into evidence-backed ScientificClaims scoped to hierarchical StudyContexts and retrieves only context-compatible evidence before path search.
4. The immediate output is an auditable hypothesis or small candidate set for later WRF/field evaluation, not an autonomous land-management prescription.
5. A shared-corpus comparison with PlainRAG and a Microsoft-GraphRAG community adapter measures expected-Claim retrieval, cited-answer coverage, condition retention, and traceability on ten fixed questions.

## Section Outline

- **Abstract:** task, context-dependence challenge, representation/query insight, measured pilot, boundary on recommendations.
- **Introduction:** climate relevance, cost of scenario simulation, why ordinary topical retrieval is insufficient, contributions.
- **Methodology:** corpus; staged/combined indexing; graph representation; context-gated query; fair baseline definitions.
- **Results:** ten-paper extraction/provenance audit; ten-query three-system comparison; heterogeneity and seasonal-regime examples; failure analysis.
- **Impact and limitations:** scientist/planner/user workflows, candidate-raster-to-WRF pathway, corpus and evaluation limits.
