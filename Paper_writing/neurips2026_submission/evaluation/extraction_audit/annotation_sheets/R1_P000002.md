- [ ] `R1_P000002:P000002_C002` CMIP6 GCM local effects from idealized deforestation simulations; location: Global
  - `spatial_configuration` / Idealized global deforestation scenario: The simulation assumes a total forest area of 20 million km² is linearly removed from the top 30% forested area globally at a fixed rate of 400,000 km² yr⁻¹ over 50 years, followed by at least 30 years with constant land cover to achieve stable conditions.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Local effect extraction method: Local effects are isolated from total GCM signals using a chessboard-like method. Non-local effects in deforested grids are interpolated from surrounding unaltered forest cover grids, and the local effect is derived by subtracting this interpolated non-local signal from the total effect.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Multi-model ensemble mean: Results represent the ensemble mean of five GCMs participating in CMIP6. Diagnostics from individual models are bilinearly remapped to a 2°×2° grid and vertically to 27 pressure levels from 1000 to 100 hPa for consistent comparison.
  - `land_surface` / Deforestation land cover change: The setting involves large-scale deforestation characterized by changes in surface albedo, evapotranspiration, and turbulent heat fluxes due to the removal of forest cover, specifically comparing deforest-glob simulations against pre-industrial control (piControl) runs.
  - `atmosphere` / Cloud profile adjustments: The atmospheric condition involves adjustments in cloud vertical structures, specifically local decreases in global low-level clouds and tropical high-level clouds resulting from deforestation, as derived from the ensemble mean of local effects.
  - `atmosphere` / Radiative forcing changes: The setting includes alterations in radiative forcing where decreased cloud cover warms the climate, partially counteracting the cooling effects of increased surface albedo. The offset ratio is approximately 44% for the CMIP6 ensemble mean.
- [ ] `R1_P000002:P000002_C003` ERA5 reanalysis with MODIS land cover via space-for-time substitution; location: Global
  - `land_surface` / MODIS land cover classification: Land cover is defined using MODIS MCD12C1 version 6.1 data (IGBP classification) from 2001 to 2021 at 0.05° resolution. Five forest types are merged into a single forest class, and the forest fraction is bilinearly gridded to 0.25° resolution to align with ERA5 data.
  - `atmosphere` / ERA5 reanalysis atmospheric variables: Atmospheric conditions are derived from ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis data spanning 2001 to 2021. The dataset includes cloud fraction profiles, surface latent and sensible heat fluxes, temperature, radiation fluxes, evapotranspiration, wind speed, and TOA radiation at 0.25° spatial resolution and 28 vertical pressure levels from 1000 to 100 hPa.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Space-for-time substitution sampling method: The study employs a space-for-time substitution method comparing unaltered forested pixels (initial tree cover >60%, net change <10%) with unaltered open land pixels (tree cover <40%, net change <10%). Pixels with water coverage >10% are excluded. A moving window of 7x7 pixels (1.75° x 1.75°) is used to identify comparison samples.
  - `substrate_terrain` / Topographic flatness constraint: To reduce the influence of topography, samples are omitted if the standard deviation of elevation within the 7x7 pixel moving window exceeds 100 meters.
  - `atmosphere` / Diurnal observation bias in cloud data: The analysis acknowledges that MODIS provides data only for specific times within the diurnal cycle (morning and noon), which may introduce a low bias in the estimate of forest-cloud impacts compared to model analysis.
