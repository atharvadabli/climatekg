## Complete Study Settings

- [ ] `R1_P000006:P000006_C001` Global afternoon precipitation-soil moisture feedback analysis framework; location: Global land surface (60°S–60°N)
  - `spatial_configuration` / Global land surface domain extent: The analysis covers the global land surface between latitudes 60°S and 60°N. Mountainous areas with topographic height variability exceeding 300 m, coastal areas, and regions containing water bodies are excluded from the analysis.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Exclusion of tropical forest regions: Tropical forest regions are excluded from the observational analysis because accurate soil moisture retrievals are unavailable beneath dense vegetation.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Exclusion of strong soil moisture gradients: Regions containing strong climatological soil moisture gradients are excluded from the analysis to ensure robust comparison of local anomalies.
  - `atmosphere` / Afternoon precipitation event timing: Precipitation events are defined by maximum accumulated rainfall occurring between 12:00 and 21:00 local time. The analysis focuses on daytime moist convection when sensitivity to land conditions is maximized.
  - `atmosphere` / Precipitation intensity threshold: An afternoon precipitation event is identified when accumulated precipitation exceeds 3 mm. Pixels with more than 1 mm of rain in the preceding hours are excluded to ensure soil moisture measurements precede rainfall.
  - `land_surface` / Soil moisture measurement timing: Surface soil moisture retrievals are used with overpasses typically at 1:30 or 13:30 for AMSR-E, and 9:30 or 21:30 for ASCAT. Soil moisture data from 13:30 is filtered to remove cases close to active precipitation.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Event detection spatial scale: Precipitation events are defined at a 0.25° × 0.25° pixel location (L_max) centered in a box measuring 1.25° × 1.25°. This corresponds to a spatial scale of approximately 50–100 kilometers.
- [ ] `R1_P000006:P000006_C002` Satellite observational dataset analysis; location: Global land surface (60°S–60°N)
  - `land_surface` / Satellite soil moisture datasets and temporal coverage: Surface soil moisture retrievals are derived from the Advanced Microwave Scanning Radiometer for EOS (AMSR-E) covering June 2002 to October 2011, and the MetOP Advanced Scatterometer (ASCAT) covering 2007–2011. These datasets provide typically one overpass per pixel per day.
  - `atmosphere` / Satellite precipitation dataset source: Precipitation data are obtained from the CMORPH 3-hourly dataset, which is based on data from a combination of satellites.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Dataset quality selection methodology: For each 5° × 5° cell, the relative quality of the ASCAT and AMSR-E soil moisture datasets is tested independently to determine which product is used for the analysis.
- [ ] `R1_P000006:P000006_C003` Global climate and reanalysis model ensemble analysis; location: Global land surface (60°S–60°N)
  - `spatial_configuration` / Model ensemble horizontal resolution range: The analysis utilizes six global models ranging in horizontal resolution from 0.5 to 2.0 degrees. This coarser resolution compared to observations affects the ability to resolve mesoscale soil moisture structures.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Reduced event detection box size for models: Due to the lower spatial resolution of the models (0.5–2.0 degrees), the event detection box is reduced from 5x5 pixels to 3x3 pixels when analyzing model diagnostics.
  - `atmosphere` / Adjusted soil moisture sampling time window for models: For model analysis, the local time window between 6:00 and 8:59 is adopted to calculate soil moisture differences. This timing accounts for diurnal phase bias in model precipitation.
  - `atmosphere` / Extended convective rain accumulation period for models: Convective rain is accumulated over the subsequent 9 hours in model analysis. This duration is several hours earlier than observational windows to account for diurnal phase bias in simulated precipitation.
  - `other` / Specific global models included in ensemble: The model ensemble consists of four climate models (HadGEM2, CNRM-CM5, MRI-AGCM3-2H, INMCM4) and two atmospheric reanalysis models (MERRA, ERA-Interim).
