## Complete Study Settings

- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C001` Global CAM3-CLM3.5 land-atmosphere simulation framework; location: Global domain
  - `spatial_configuration` / Global domain resolution: The simulation framework operates on a global domain. The land model data were regridded to T42 spectral resolution, corresponding to approximately 2.8° × 2.8° grid cells, which was used for the climate model simulations.
  - `atmosphere` / Atmospheric model configuration: The Community Atmosphere Model (CAM) version 3 was used with a spectral Eulerian dynamical core. The vertical structure consisted of 26 levels, and the time step for coupled runs was set to 20 minutes.
  - `land_surface` / Land model version and processes: Version 3.5 of the Community Land Model (CLM) was employed. It models solar and longwave radiation interactions, momentum and heat fluxes, soil and snow hydrology with ten layers totaling 3.4 m depth, and stomatal physiology.
  - `land_surface` / Vegetation and soil column structure: Each grid cell contains multiple plant functional types (PFTs) derived from MODIS and historical crop maps. To prevent unrealistic irrigation of natural vegetation, the soil in each grid cell was divided into two separate columns: one for crops and one for natural PFTs.
  - `climate` / Sea surface temperature forcing: Climatological sea surface temperatures were used instead of an interactive ocean model to decrease interannual variability and increase the signal-to-noise ratio of irrigation effects on climate.
  - `other` / Initial conditions and spin-up: Model initial conditions were taken from a 150-year spin-up simulation to ensure a near-equilibrium initial state before running the specific study scenarios.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C002` Interactive atmosphere-land coupled simulations
  - `other` / Simulation duration and spin-up protocol: Two 30-year coupled simulations were performed. The first nine years of each simulation were discarded as additional spin-up to account for initialization differences, with comparisons based on the last 21 years.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C003` Offline land model simulations driven by observations
  - `other` / Simulation duration and spin-up protocol: Each offline simulation was run for ten years starting from a spun-up state. The first three years were discarded as additional spin-up, and differences were computed averaged over the last seven years.
  - `atmosphere` / Atmospheric forcing data source and resolution: The simulations used forcing data from Qian et al. (2006) for the years 1995–2004. These data merge monthly observations with intramonthly anomalies from NCEP-NCAR reanalysis, resulting in a 3-hour temporal resolution.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Offline simulation time step: The spatial resolution of the offline runs was the same as that of the coupled runs (roughly 2.8° × 2.8°), but the time step was set to 30 minutes, in contrast to the 20-minute time step used for the coupled runs.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C004` Coupled control simulation without irrigation
  - `other` / Simulation duration and analysis period: The coupled control simulation ran for 30 years. The first nine years were discarded as additional spin-up to account for initialization differences, with comparisons based on the last 21 years of the simulation.
  - `land_surface` / Soil column configuration for crops: The soil in each grid cell was divided into two separate columns: one for crops and one for natural plant functional types. This modification was applied to the control run to prevent unrealistic irrigation of natural vegetation.
  - `land_surface` / Absence of irrigation forcing: The control simulation was performed without any irrigation applied to the land surface, serving as the baseline for comparison with the coupled irrigation simulation.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C005` Coupled simulation with irrigation applied over 24 hours
  - `hydrology` / Irrigation timing protocol: Irrigation was applied continuously over a 24-hour period in each grid cell. The application occurred whenever the crop leaf area index (LAI) reached at least 80% of its maximum annual value, corresponding to the peak of the growing season.
  - `hydrology` / Total annual irrigation volume: The simulation distributed approximately 2,560 km³ of water to global agricultural lands annually. This volume is equivalent to 17 mm of water spread evenly over the land surface, representing about 2.1% of the precipitation over land in the control run.
  - `climate` / Seasonal distribution of irrigation: Northern mid-latitude irrigation occurred primarily in boreal summer (June, July, August). Irrigation in India and much of Southeast Asia occurred mainly in autumn (September, October, November). Less irrigation occurred in spring, and even less in winter.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C006` Offline control simulation without irrigation and separate crop soil column
  - `land_surface` / Separate crop soil column configuration: The simulation employed a separate soil column for crops within each grid cell, distinct from the columns used for natural vegetation. This configuration was applied to prevent the unrealistic irrigation of natural vegetation types that occurs when all plant functional types share a single soil column.
  - `other` / Absence of irrigation forcing: This specific simulation run served as a control scenario and did not include any irrigation water application. It was designed to provide a baseline for comparison against irrigated offline simulations, maintaining the same soil column structure but without the added water input.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C007` Offline control simulation with single shared soil column
  - `land_surface` / Single shared soil column configuration: In this simulation, cropland and natural vegetation within each grid cell share a single soil column. Consequently, if irrigation occurs anywhere in the grid cell, it is spread evenly over all vegetation types, rather than being restricted to crops.
  - `land_surface` / Irrigation application to non-crop vegetation: Grid cells where the irrigation dataset indicated irrigation but CLM input data contained no crops were irrigated in this run. This occurred because the single shared soil column simplified the modeling, allowing irrigation to apply even where specific crop PFTs were absent.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C008` Offline simulation with irrigation applied over 24 hours and separate crop soil column
  - `land_surface` / Irrigation timing and distribution: Irrigation was applied evenly throughout the day, with a small amount occurring in each time step. This contrasts with pulse irrigation scenarios where water is applied in concentrated bursts.
  - `land_surface` / Irrigated area extent: The simulation irrigated an area of 1.35 × 10^7 km². This represents the area where irrigation was applied over the separate crop soil column, distinct from runs with shared soil columns which had larger irrigated areas.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C009` Offline simulation with irrigation applied over 24 hours and single shared soil column
  - `land_surface` / Single shared soil column configuration: Unlike the standard separate crop soil column setup, this simulation used a single shared soil column for all vegetation types in each grid cell. Consequently, if irrigation occurred anywhere in a grid cell, it was spread evenly over all vegetation, including natural PFTs.
  - `spatial_configuration` / Expanded irrigated area extent: Due to the shared soil column configuration, the irrigated area in this run was nearly seven times larger than in the standard offline irrigation run (O-IRRIG). The total global irrigation volume remained approximately the same as in other runs.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C010` Offline simulation with irrigation pulse at midnight
  - `other` / Irrigation timing and pulse duration: Irrigation water was applied in a concentrated pulse for one hour each day, starting at midnight local time. The total annual irrigation volume remained the same as in the standard offline irrigation run.
- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_C011` Offline simulation with irrigation pulse at noon
  - `other` / Irrigation timing and pulse duration: Irrigation water was applied in a concentrated pulse for one hour each day, starting at noon local time. The total annual irrigation volume remained the same as in the standard runs.

- [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_CL002` global irrigation application (presence) --causal--> ground evaporation (increase): Irrigation caused a global annual increase in ground evaporation of 0.556 W m-2, accounting for the majority of the total latent heat flux increase. - [ ] `R1_P000003:P000003_CL004` global irrigation application (presence) --causal--> near-surface specific humidity (increase): Irrigation increased near-surface specific humidity over land by 0.767% annually, with larger increases observed during boreal summer and autumn.
