\section{Full extraction audit}
\label{app:extraction}

Ten papers annotated by hand from the exported SourceBlock text, using the counting
rules of the extraction prompts so that reference and system objects are the same kind
of thing. Study settings are one per complete combination of conditions the paper
directly analyses. Claims are one per directed own-result relationship; only
\texttt{OWN\_RESULT} and \texttt{AUTHORS\_INTERPRETATION\_OF\_OWN\_RESULT} count,
because the pipeline discards the other two evidence roles. \emph{Main} findings are
those foregrounded in the abstract or numbered conclusions, plus every sign reversal,
threshold, and stated condition that changes when a main finding applies.

\section{Benchmark corpus}
\label{app:corpus}

The frozen 18-paper subset covers wind farms, deforestation and cloud cover, global
and regional irrigation, mesoscale vegetation heterogeneity, soil-moisture--rainfall
coupling, cropland intensification, large-scale renewable installations, forest cloud
enhancement, mid-latitude afforestation, broad-leaved species composition, land-use
change in South America, global greening, cover crops, observed Amazonian
deforestation, LULC warming in eastern India, and land-surface heterogeneity with
background wind. Full identifiers, titles, and per-paper object counts accompany the
released graph.

\section{Reproduction}
\label{app:repro}

The shared corpus, the ClimateKG graph, the ten-question benchmark, the extraction
ground truth, and the collation scripts are released with the code. The corpus is
assembled by \texttt{scripts/build\_three\_system\_corpus.py}, ClimateKG queries run
through \texttt{scripts/run\_three\_system\_queries.py}, the two baselines through
\texttt{baselines/climatekg\_comparison/run.py} with a \texttt{--think} flag that
matches the ClimateKG synthesis budget, results are collated by
\texttt{scripts/report\_three\_system\_comparison.py}, and the extraction audit is
recomputed by \texttt{scripts/audit\_extraction\_recall.py}. Every number in this paper
is produced by one of those scripts from saved run artifacts.
