# Query architecture benchmark: ten questions

## Purpose

This experiment compares six retrieval systems on the same ten answerable scientific questions:

1. PlainRAG
2. Microsoft GraphRAG
3. the previous ClimateKG query pipeline
4. ClimateKG with independent Claim and SourceBlock candidate retrieval (`evidence_union`)
5. ClimateKG with evidence union and soft, diversified Context matching (`soft_context`)
6. ClimateKG with soft Context matching and evidence-seeded graph expansion (`graph_expansion`)

The corpus contains 115 papers, 1,221 StudyContexts, and 1,724 Claims. The three experimental ClimateKG variants reuse the same saved QuerySpec for each question. This removes query-parser variation from the architecture comparison. All final answers were generated by the same local Qwen model.

The detailed machine-readable record is in:

`evaluation/output/query_architecture_benchmark_10/comparison.json`

It contains every question, reference answer, generated answer, metric, and a compact retrieval trace. Full per-query artifacts remain under `evaluation/output/query_architecture_benchmark_10/variants/`.

## Aggregate results

| System | Complete | Expected paper hit | Mean token F1 | Mean qualifier coverage | Median latency (s) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| PlainRAG | 10/10 | 10/10 | 0.3611 | 0.3717 | 26.0 |
| Microsoft GraphRAG | 10/10 | 7/10 | 0.2237 | 0.3100 | 35.9 |
| ClimateKG baseline | 10/10 | 6/10 | 0.2212 | 0.2650 | 185.3 |
| ClimateKG evidence union | 10/10 | 6/10 | 0.2146 | 0.2600 | 180.9 |
| ClimateKG soft context | 10/10 | 6/10 | **0.2370** | **0.2800** | 183.8 |
| ClimateKG graph expansion | 10/10 | 6/10 | 0.2293 | 0.2800 | 186.2 |

Token F1 and qualifier coverage are diagnostic lexical measures. They do not establish scientific correctness by themselves. Expected-paper hit checks whether evidence from the paper used to construct the reference answer reached synthesis.

## Did ClimateKG improve?

Soft Context matching is the best of the tested ClimateKG variants. Relative to the baseline, its mean token F1 increases from 0.2212 to 0.2370 and mean qualifier coverage increases from 0.2650 to 0.2800. It does not improve expected-paper recall, which remains 6/10.

Evidence union by itself does not improve the aggregate result. It helps expose Claims that lack a shortlisted Context, but it cannot recover a finding that was never extracted as a Claim.

Graph expansion is not accepted. It increases the number of paths for CKGQ0009 from 4 to 20, but none reaches the requested fire-intensity finding. The answer still abstains. On average it performs below soft Context matching and is slightly slower. The added paths are related to Amazon climate processes but do not answer the question.

PlainRAG is strongest on this benchmark. These questions ask for findings located in specific source passages. Direct passage retrieval can recover those passages even when structured extraction omitted the corresponding Claim. This does not test the main advantage expected from ClimateKG: combining compatible evidence across papers for context-dependent transfer or multi-step reasoning.

The best ClimateKG variant therefore produces four substantially correct answers, two partial answers, three grounded abstentions, and one incorrect answer in this small manual review. Abstention is preferable to an unsupported answer, but it is still a task failure because all ten questions are answerable from indexed source text.

## Root causes

### 1. Structured extraction recall is the main limit

Several gold passages exist in the indexed SourceBlocks but have no equivalent Claim:

- P000100: wettest versus driest years and the greater-than-20% IGP contribution;
- P000085: the forest/grassland sensible-heat reversal during progressive drought and southwest-Australia seasonal albedo values;
- P000122: reduced evapotranspirative cooling under cold or near-saturated high-humidity conditions;
- P000103: the regional drivers of future fire intensity;
- P000120: the exact 74% versus 49% hot-temperature frequencies.

Changing Context gates or adding graph traversal cannot retrieve a missing structured fact.

### 2. Claim ranking can omit a needed companion Claim

P000040 contains both September irrigation effects and ENSO-conditioned effects. The query needs both groups, but the final evidence budget favors the ENSO Claims and omits the September Claims. This is a ranking and evidence-coverage problem, not an extraction problem.

### 3. Context diversification helps only when the needed Claim exists

For CKGQ0002, soft Context matching prevents near-duplicate Contexts from occupying the shortlist and gives the correct P000064 evidence an applicable Context. This is the intended improvement. It cannot solve CKGQ0001, CKGQ0003, CKGQ0005, or CKGQ0009 because the required Claim is absent or the correct paper never reaches the evidence set.

### 4. Unconstrained graph expansion adds topical rather than answering paths

Only CKGQ0009 materially changes its graph-path count. The additional paths concern related Amazon processes but do not reach fire intensity. Exact shared-State connectivity is therefore insufficient as an expansion criterion.

## Decision

Keep `soft_context` as the preferred experimental query architecture. Do not merge `graph_expansion` in its current form.

The next minimal experiment should add a provenance-preserving SourceBlock evidence lane beside structured Claim retrieval. It should retrieve raw SourceBlocks against the complete question, retain their paper/page/block IDs, and use them only when they directly cover a query component that the selected Claims do not cover. Structured Claims remain responsible for Context gating and path reasoning; the SourceBlock lane prevents extraction omissions from making indexed evidence unreachable.

This is different from weakening Context gating or indiscriminately appending passages. Selection should be based on explicit query-component coverage, with a small fixed evidence budget and a visible reason for each admitted block.

## Reproduction

Run one variant:

```powershell
.\.venv-climatekg\Scripts\python.exe scripts\run_query_architecture_benchmark.py `
  --project-root . `
  --code-root climatekg\runtime\worktrees\query-soft-context `
  --variant soft_context `
  --graph-root climatekg\runtime\outputs\three_system_benchmark\parquet_graph `
  --dataset evaluation\output\climatekg_benchmark\dataset_v1_draft\cases.json `
  --output evaluation\output\query_architecture_benchmark_10
```

Rebuild the combined comparison:

```powershell
.\.venv-climatekg\Scripts\python.exe scripts\summarize_query_architecture_benchmark.py `
  --project-root . `
  --output evaluation\output\query_architecture_benchmark_10
```
