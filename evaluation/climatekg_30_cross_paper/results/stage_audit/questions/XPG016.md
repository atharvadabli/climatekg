## Question

Why can deforestation warm tropical surfaces while producing cooling in some boreal or global-mean experiments? Separate the latent-heat, roughness, albedo, snow, and circulation pathways.

Reference expectation:

Tropical deforestation often warms locally because reduced evapotranspiration and roughness weaken latent cooling. At high latitudes, increased albedo, especially with snow, can dominate and cool the surface; atmospheric and ocean circulation then redistribute these responses, so global means can differ from local tropical effects.
