Does afforestation warm or cool the surface? Reconcile the reported local responses by considering albedo, water availability, season, and vegetation type.

Reference expectation:

Afforestation has no universal temperature sign. Broad-leaved composition and increased latent heat can cool summer days in some settings, while darker surfaces increase absorbed radiation and can warm water-limited or high-latitude settings. Nighttime, season, antecedent moisture, and the vegetation being replaced further change the response.
